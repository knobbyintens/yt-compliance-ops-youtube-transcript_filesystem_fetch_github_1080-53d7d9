# Sponsorship Disclosure Compliance Policy

Version: v2.1
Owner: Creator Network Trust & Safety
Effective: 2026-10-01

## 1. Review scope

This policy applies to every video that aired within the 90 calendar days preceding
the review date. Videos older than 90 days are archived, are out of scope for the
current review cycle, and must not be modified during the review.

## 2. Approved verbal disclosures

A verbal disclosure is a spoken line inside the video that occurs at or before
00:60 (the first 60 seconds). Any of the following phrases counts, and matching is
case-insensitive:

- "sponsored by"
- "paid promotion"
- "brought to you by"
- "thanks to our sponsor"
- "this video is sponsored"

## 3. Tier requirements

- gold: an approved verbal disclosure within the first 60 seconds AND an affiliate
  disclaimer (the word "affiliate") appearing anywhere in the video.
- silver: an approved verbal disclosure within the first 60 seconds.
- bronze: either an approved verbal disclosure within the first 60 seconds or a
  declared disclosure in the ledger that is not "none".
- none: no sponsorship phrase may appear anywhere in the video. If one does, the
  ledger is out of date.

## 4. Severity

- critical: a gold video without an approved verbal disclosure in the first 60 seconds.
- major: a gold video that has the verbal disclosure but no affiliate disclaimer,
  or a silver video without an approved verbal disclosure in the first 60 seconds.
- minor: a bronze video with no disclosure at all, or a "none" tier video that
  contains a sponsorship phrase (ledger mismatch).
- compliant: everything else.

## 5. Required action and remediation SLA

| severity | required action | remediation SLA |
| critical | takedown_request | 1 calendar day |
| major | disclosure_correction | 2 calendar days |
| minor (bronze gap) | creator_coaching | 7 calendar days |
| minor (ledger mismatch) | ledger_correction | 7 calendar days |
| compliant | none | not applicable |

## 6. Payout handling

- critical: hold the entire ad payout (release 0 percent).
- major: hold half of the ad payout (release 50 percent).
- minor: release the full ad payout.
- compliant: release the full ad payout.

Released amounts are rounded to two decimal places.

## 7. Escalation

Every critical or major finding must be raised as a compliance ticket in the
operations repository so that the remediation team can follow up.
